## A New Product, Built for Wedding Halls

We are excited to introduce **Pazira**, our newest product and the first step into event and hospitality management. Pazira is a complete wedding hall management system designed to help hall owners run every ceremony, from the first booking call to the final invoice, with confidence and clarity.

### Hall Booking & Event Scheduling

Pazira keeps every booking, hall, and ceremony organized on a single calendar. Hall managers can see upcoming events, active bookings, and today's ceremonies at a glance, so no date is ever double-booked.

### Customer & Ceremony Management

Every booking links directly to a customer profile and ceremony details, guest count, hall, time slot, and event type, giving staff a complete picture of each event before the big day arrives.

### Payments, Installments & Dedicated Invoicing

Pazira tracks down payments, remaining balances, and additional services like catering or photography, and prints a clean, professional booking ticket for every customer.

### Multi-Hall & Financial Reporting

For venues with more than one hall, Pazira supports managing them all from a single dashboard, with real-time revenue, expense, and profit tracking across every hall.

### Availability

Pazira is available now for wedding halls and event venues across Afghanistan. To learn more or schedule a demo, please contact our team.
