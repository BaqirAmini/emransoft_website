## Labra Keeps Getting Better

We are pleased to announce a new round of updates to Labra, our dental laboratory management system. This release focuses on clearer client communication, better reporting, and — for the first time — full bilingual support.

### Now Available in English and Dari

Labra is now fully bilingual. Previously offered in Dari only, the entire interface can now be used in English as well. This makes Labra accessible to a wider range of technicians and laboratories and easier to adopt for teams that prefer to work in either language.

### Dedicated, Professional Printing for Every Clinic

Labra can now generate dedicated, professionally formatted printouts for each client clinic. Instead of generic documents, every clinic receives clean, well-organized reports and invoices — reinforcing your laboratory's professional image with each delivery.

### Group Cases by Tooth Within the Same Mould

On the order page, cases can now be grouped by tooth within a single mould. This makes multi-unit work far easier to enter and track: related units are organized together under the same mould instead of being scattered across separate entries, reducing errors and saving time.

### A Smarter Dashboard

The dashboard now shows the total number of units alongside the number of cases, giving managers a more accurate picture of workload and output. Both figures can be filtered by time period, so you can review activity for any day, week, or month at a glance.

### And Many More Improvements

Beyond these highlights, this release brings a range of additional refinements across the interface, along with performance and stability improvements throughout Labra — all designed to make your daily workflow faster and smoother.

### Availability

These updates are available now for all Labra users. Existing customers can upgrade by contacting our support team, and all of your existing data is fully preserved.
